IEE or EIA: which one does your project need?

An Initial Environmental Examination (IEE) is a shorter study for projects with moderate impact. An Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) is a deeper study for projects with bigger impact. Which one applies depends on the category the Department of Environment gives your project.

Why does a factory need an ETP?

An effluent treatment plant cleans industrial wastewater before it is released. This protects rivers, soil and nearby communities, and it is required to get and keep environmental clearance.
