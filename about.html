About us

An environmental research and consultancy firm based in Gazipur.

Who we are

BlueGreen Research and Consultancy started in June 2026 in Gazipur. Our team combines engineering, environmental science and GIS skills to help industries meet environmental requirements and to help students and researchers do better research.

Mission

To provide reliable, practical environmental consultancy and research support that helps industries operate responsibly and helps researchers grow.

Vision

A Bangladesh where industry and nature thrive together, backed by sound science and skilled people.

Values

Integrity, scientific accuracy, and respect for people and the environment.

Why BlueGreen

Academic roots

Our team works close to the university research community at DUET, Gazipur.

Practical focus

We deliver designs, reports and training that people can actually use.

Support from start to finish

From first idea to the final report or published paper.
